## 🐾 What Is a PetPet GIF?

A PetPet GIF, also known as a headpat GIF or patting GIF, is a popular internet meme format featuring a hand gently patting the head of a character, animal, or other image subject.

The animation usually consists of:

1. A hand descending toward the subject's head.
2. Gentle contact with the subject.
3. A subtle squishing or compression effect.
4. The subject returning to its original shape.
5. The motion repeating in a seamless loop.

The result is a wholesome, funny, and expressive animation that communicates affection, encouragement, approval, or appreciation.

PetPet memes are especially popular in anime communities, gaming communities, Discord servers, Reddit, and social media.
